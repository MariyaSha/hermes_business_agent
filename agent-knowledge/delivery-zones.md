# DragonDash Delivery Zones

DragonDash currently offers standard service in five major Bulgarian cities and selected destinations across Westeros.

## Bulgaria
Standard pickup and delivery are available in:

1. **Sofia**
2. **Plovdiv**
3. **Varna**
4. **Burgas**
5. **Ruse**

Deliveries between these cities qualify for the standard intercity rate when booked in the regular 3–5 business day window.

## Westeros
Approved cross-realm destinations:

- **King's Landing**
- **Winterfell**
- **Dragonstone**
- **The Eyrie**
- **Casterly Rock**

Westeros deliveries use the published cross-realm base rate, but weather, war, ravens, castle access, and dragon restrictions may affect timing.

## Outside Our Standard Zones
Other locations may still be possible.

For villages, remote areas, islands, mountain locations, restricted castles, or destinations not listed above, contact a DragonDash representative for a special quote.
