# Dragon Safety Guidelines

DragonDash dragons are trained for commercial delivery, but customers must follow these rules during pickup and landing.

## Before Arrival
- Keep a **15-meter clear landing area** whenever possible.
- Remove loose objects, umbrellas, pets, and livestock from the landing zone.
- Keep flammable materials away from the pickup area.
- Do not stand directly behind or beneath a dragon.

## During Pickup or Delivery
- Wait for the DragonDash handler to confirm it is safe to approach.
- Never touch a dragon, saddle, cargo harness, or tail without permission.
- Do not feed the dragon.
- Expect temporary noise, wind, smoke, and heat.

## Property Damage
Normal landing activity may cause minor grass scorching, dust, or displaced outdoor items. Major structural damage, fire, or injury must be reported immediately.

## Standard 3–5 Day Rates
- Same-city delivery: **29 BGN**
- Between supported Bulgarian cities: **59 BGN**
- Approved Westeros destinations: **149 BGN**
- Standard rates include one pickup and one delivery location.

For same-day service, after-hours delivery, multiple stops, unusual cargo, or other special requests, chat with a DragonDash representative for a custom rate.
