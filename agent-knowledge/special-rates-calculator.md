# DragonDash Special Rates Calculator

Use this guide to estimate non-standard delivery pricing. Final special rates may be adjusted by a DragonDash representative.

## Step 1 — Start With the Base Rate
- Same city: **29 BGN**
- Supported Bulgarian city-to-city: **59 BGN**
- Approved Westeros destination: **149 BGN**

## Step 2 — Add Special-Service Adjustments

### Extra Stops
The base rate includes one pickup and one delivery.

- Each additional stop: **+20 BGN**
- 4 or more total stops: add **10%** to the full delivery price

### Distance
For routes outside standard city-to-city coverage:

- First 100 km included
- Each additional kilometer: **+0.40 BGN/km**

### Speed
- Same-day delivery: **×1.75**
- Delivery within 4 hours: **×2.25**
- Priority emergency dispatch: **×3.00**

### Pickup / Delivery Time
- Before 09:00 or after 17:00: **×1.25**
- Between 22:00 and 06:00: **×1.50**
- Weekend or public holiday: **×1.25**

Use only the highest applicable time multiplier.

### Special Handling
- Fragile or fire-sensitive cargo: **+25 BGN**
- Oversized cargo: **+40 BGN**
- Restricted-access location: **+50 BGN**
- Reinforced landing support required: **+75 BGN**

## Calculation Order
1. Start with the base rate.
2. Add distance, extra-stop, and handling charges.
3. Apply the speed multiplier.
4. Apply the time multiplier.
5. Apply the 10% multi-stop surcharge if there are 4+ total stops.

## Example
A 59 BGN intercity delivery with one extra stop (+20 BGN), same-day service (×1.75), and evening delivery (×1.25):

**(59 + 20) × 1.75 × 1.25 = 172.81 BGN**

Round the customer estimate to **173 BGN**.

For unusual cases, uncertain routes, or conflicting conditions, do not guess — escalate to a DragonDash representative.
