# DragonDash Delivery — Company Overview

DragonDash Delivery is a fast, dragon-powered courier and dispatch company serving major cities in Bulgaria and selected destinations across Westeros.

## What We Deliver
- Parcels, documents, retail orders, and business supplies
- Same-day and scheduled deliveries
- Multi-stop business routes
- Special handling for fragile or fire-sensitive cargo
- Cross-realm deliveries to approved Westeros destinations

## Standard Service
Standard delivery takes **3–5 business days** and uses regular published rates.

Special requests — including same-day delivery, after-hours pickup, multi-stop routes, long-distance jobs, or unusual cargo — are quoted separately by a DragonDash representative.

## Customer Support
Our support team can:
- Quote standard deliveries
- Explain delivery zones and policies
- Help reschedule pickups or deliveries
- Open damage or delay cases
- Calculate special-rate estimates
- Escalate serious incidents to a human dispatcher

## Important
Dragons are trained professionals, but they remain dragons. Minor smoke, noise, wing turbulence, and scorched grass may occur during normal operations.
