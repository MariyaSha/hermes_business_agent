# DragonDash Opening Hours

## Standard Service Hours
**Monday–Friday: 09:00–17:00**

Standard published rates apply to pickups and deliveries scheduled within these hours.

Standard delivery time is **3–5 business days**.

## 24/7 Special Service
DragonDash accepts urgent and special deliveries **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**.

This includes:
- Same-day delivery
- Evening or overnight pickup
- Weekend delivery
- Emergency business deliveries
- Multi-stop routes
- Cross-realm urgent deliveries

24/7 jobs use special pricing. Customers should chat with a DragonDash representative for an estimate.

## Customer Support
Automated customer support is available **24/7**.

Requests requiring a human dispatcher may be handled during standard business hours unless the delivery is marked urgent.
